# Positioning: THE THIRD PILLAR vs Existing Work

**Mechanical differences only. No marketing. No critique of tone.**

## GovSim

**What GovSim measures:**
- Policy outcomes in multi-agent governance scenarios
- Voting behavior, coalition formation
- Institutional design effects

**What GovSim assumes:**
- Agents have full information about others' actions
- Communication is costless and unambiguous
- Time is synchronous (all agents act simultaneously)

**What THE THIRD PILLAR isolates that GovSim does not:**
- **Information asymmetry**: Epistemic orders create temporal and observational constraints
- **Language as constraint**: Linguistic regimes mechanically restrict what can be expressed
- **Derived dilemmas**: Social dilemmas emerge from resource dynamics, not scripted payoffs
- **Evolution-first**: Biology and environment generate constraints, not abstract game theory

## Melting Pot

**What Melting Pot measures:**
- Emergent cooperation in multi-agent reinforcement learning
- Population-level behavioral patterns
- Training dynamics and generalization

**What Melting Pot assumes:**
- Agents learn via RL (policy gradients, value functions)
- Rewards are explicitly defined
- Observation spaces are fixed and known

**What THE THIRD PILLAR isolates that Melting Pot does not:**
- **LLM policies**: Agents are controlled by language models, not learned policies
- **Language constraints**: Communication is mechanically restricted, not learned
- **Epistemic asymmetry**: Agents operate on different time scales and observation bandwidths
- **Institutional constraints**: Treaties impose machine-readable constraints, not learned norms

## Biosphere / ALife Sims

**What Biosphere measures:**
- Evolutionary dynamics in artificial ecosystems
- Emergent behaviors from simple rules
- Long-term stability and diversity

**What Biosphere assumes:**
- Agents are simple (genetic algorithms, cellular automata)
- Selection pressure is environmental
- No explicit communication or negotiation

**What THE THIRD PILLAR isolates that Biosphere does not:**
- **Strategic intelligence**: Agents use LLMs, not simple heuristics
- **Institutional formation**: Treaties and constraints are explicitly modeled
- **Language-mediated cooperation**: Communication constraints affect outcomes
- **Capability scaling**: Direct comparison across LLM capability levels

## Sentient Struggle–style Arenas

**What Sentient Struggle measures:**
- Strategic behavior in competitive scenarios
- Exploitation vs cooperation
- Model capability effects on outcomes

**What Sentient Struggle assumes:**
- Payoff matrices are fixed
- Agents have full information
- Time is discrete and synchronous

**What THE THIRD PILLAR isolates that Sentient Struggle does not:**
- **Resource dynamics**: Payoffs emerge from resource regeneration and collapse
- **Information constraints**: Epistemic orders create asymmetric information
- **Language constraints**: Communication is mechanically restricted
- **Evolutionary pressure**: Population dynamics create selection pressure

## Core Isolation

**THE THIRD PILLAR uniquely combines:**
1. **LLM policies** (not RL, not simple heuristics)
2. **Derived dilemmas** (not scripted payoffs)
3. **Language constraints** (mechanical, not learned)
4. **Epistemic asymmetry** (temporal and informational)
5. **Institutional formation** (treaties with machine-readable constraints)

**This combination enables testing:**
- Whether increased LLM capability expands cooperative equilibria
- Whether language constraints affect cooperation
- Whether epistemic asymmetry creates coordination failures
- Whether institutions can stabilize cooperation under information asymmetry

**What THE THIRD PILLAR does NOT do:**
- Learn policies (agents use fixed LLM policies)
- Optimize for cooperation (dilemmas are derived, not designed)
- Assume full information (epistemic orders create asymmetry)
- Use scripted payoffs (dynamics emerge from resource coupling)
